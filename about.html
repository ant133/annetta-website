About me

I'm happiest moving between interfaces, backend systems and infrastructure, then polishing the small details that make everything feel human. I like the space where design and engineering meet: where a clear system can still have warmth, motion and personality.

About this site

annetta.dev is my personal portfolio. It is told as a short story set at a public pool: you follow Annetta from the entrance, past a vending machine full of projects, through a few lanes of skills, and out again (without a towel). It is a static site built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Three.js, with no accounts and no sign-up. It works on desktop and phone.

What I build

  • Poster Buron Generator: turns a photo into a shareable poster buron, ready to post on Threads.
  • One Piece Bounty Poster: creates a custom One Piece-style bounty poster directly in the browser.
  • One Call Away: a gentle reminder app for keeping up with the people you care about.
  • Cozy Pomodoro: a calm focus timer with an animal companion.
  • Chord Analyzer: a concept for following synchronized chord diagrams while an MP3 or YouTube video plays.

Skills

Frontend, backend, infrastructure and DevOps, design, product management and AI. The home page shows them as swimming lanes.
