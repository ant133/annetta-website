Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. Applies to annetta.dev (and its GitHub Pages address), the personal portfolio of Annetta, based in Jakarta, Indonesia.

The short version

There are no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking of my own. The site does not set cookies of its own and does not store anything on your device. It shows ads through Google AdSense, and Google and its partners may use cookies for that. Details below.

What stays on your device

Nothing. The portfolio does not use cookies, localStorage or IndexedDB. Your scroll position and the project you open in the pop-up are only held in memory while the page is open.

What I collect

I do not run analytics on this site, and I do not collect names, emails or other personal data through it. If you email me, I receive your address and message, and use them only to reply.

Third parties

  • Google AdSense. The site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies, and may receive your IP address and device information, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, or opt out of personalised ads from other vendors at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you visit from these regions, a consent message from Google asks for your choice before ads that use cookies or personal data are shown. You can change your choice from the same message.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and request details when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • jsDelivr serves the three.js library used for the 3D scene, and Google Fonts serves the fonts. Your browser requests them directly, so those services see your IP address.
  • Links out. My projects (on subdomains of annetta.dev), GitHub, Threads and Instagram are separate sites with their own policies. Each project may have its own privacy policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works; ads may be less relevant.
  • Use the Google links above to control ad personalisation.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions or requests: contact me through annetta.dev.
