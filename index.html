4:16 PM · LAST LAP COMPLETE

The afternoon was beginning to soften.

Long shadows stretched across the tiles. Annetta climbed out, gathered her goggles, found both flip-flops, and checked that her laptop was still completely dry.

For one brief, shining moment, it looked as though she might leave the pool with everything under control.

Time to dry off and—…I forgot to bring my towel.

4:19 PM · A SMALL SPLASH

So much for a graceful exit.

Distracted, dripping wet, and trying to look completely normal, Annetta slipped. She stood back up, recovered what remained of her dignity, and looked toward the water.

It was still warm. The sun was still shining. And one more swim suddenly sounded much better than going home without a towel.

She turned around.
